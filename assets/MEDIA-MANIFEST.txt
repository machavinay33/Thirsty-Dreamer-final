THIRSTY DREAMER — MEDIA UPLOADS

Videos and the two portrait images are uploaded from /admin.html after the
Supabase setup in ../README.md. The CMS uses the public-read site-media bucket
for administrator uploads and stores media links with saved site content.

Supported files: MP4, WebM, MOV, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF.
Per-file limit: 50 MiB by default (subject to your Supabase project plan).
Large video files use resumable TUS uploads.

Please upload Massi-approved final edits and photographs. Temporary artwork on
the public site is intentional until finished media is supplied.
